# Top11 Control Points: surface control-net deletion — 2026-10-31

Status: PARTIAL_CHECKS_PASSED. This extends multiple control-grip deletion to typed NURBS surfaces. OM9 accepts a selection forming complete rows, complete columns or their union, removes those rows/columns and corresponding interior knots, and keeps a rectangular rational net. Individual or incomplete rows, deletions leaving too few poles for the degree, trimmed axes and periodic deleted directions are refused. The row/column requirement and knot choice are OM9 implementation decisions; exact Matrix behavior for arbitrary surface selections is not established.

Core fixtures cover U rows, V columns and their union on a rational 4x4 surface, preserved remaining poles/weights, domain/reversed metadata, valid knots and a changed evaluated surface point, plus partial-row, periodic and minimum-size refusals. A controller fixture deletes a surface row and curve pole in one transaction, verifies rollback for a partial row, Undo/Redo and `.om9` round trip. A headless UI fixture selects projected row grips and sends Delete; it does not simulate the complete mouse path.
