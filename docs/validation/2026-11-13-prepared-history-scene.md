# Prepared history candidates and peer scene construction

2026-11-13. `TransactionCoordinator` now separates history candidate preparation from publication. Preparation registers the immutable document owners with the shared account, reserves the staging map, and leaves the public world and Undo/Redo stacks unchanged. Publication checks the originating coordinator context, history head and memory account, then rechecks retained ownership under the shared account locks before changing the world and stacks. Dropping or rejecting a candidate releases its map reservation. Existing synchronous `undo_with`/`redo_with` use this path; their callback receives zero additional staged-map usage because that map is already reserved. This keeps the existing caller behavior and makes the lifetime boundary usable by a future worker.

Scene capture also accepts an immutable document already registered in its shared account, so an admission peer can construct a candidate scene. A fixture sends a prepared history candidate and a peer to a different thread, builds a scene containing a synthetic B-rep, verifies its tracked display pin, and publishes the matching candidate only after the worker returns. It separately checks simulated physical-memory refusal, discarded Undo head, a foreign account and unchanged world/stacks on refusal. Core fixtures check candidate drop, changed world, tighter managed budget, successful Undo and Redo. This is a test of the coordination boundary, not a product background Undo, a cold restore, actual OCCT reconstruction, or desktop input. Candidate documents and current/history `Brep` still own their display; the worker fixture does not establish eviction or source-only ownership.
