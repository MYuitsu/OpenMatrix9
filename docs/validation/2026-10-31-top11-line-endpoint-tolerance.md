# Top11 Split/Trim: finite line endpoint tolerance

For two nonparallel finite line segments, the previous analytic intersection branch returned no hit whenever the closest points on the infinite lines lay outside either segment. This ignored a real segment endpoint closer than the document tolerance. The constrained branch now evaluates the four endpoint-to-opposite-segment projections and uses the nearest pair, accepting it only when their measured 3D separation is within tolerance. Parallel overlap remains handled separately. The returned fractions are on the original finite curves; each output piece remains on its source curve, rather than moving an endpoint to the midpoint between the curves.

A core regression failed before the change and now passes for both operand orders and cutter directions, including an endpoint pair near both segment ends and a 3D near miss outside tolerance. Controller fixtures exercise Split and Trim through both toolbar and CMD dispatch: an endpoint 0.0005 mm from the target under a 0.001 mm tolerance gives the expected cut at x=5, with `.om9` save/open and Undo/Redo. A real 3DM export/import test checks both Split pieces at that cut. Toolbar invocation here is the controller route, not physical pointer or GPU acceptance.
