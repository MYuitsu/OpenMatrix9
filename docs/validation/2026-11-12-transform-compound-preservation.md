# Transform preserves a disjoint B-rep compound

The OCCT transform adapter previously mapped all solids or shells in the transformed result, then replaced the entire result with the first fixed solid or shell. Thus a compound with two disjoint cubes lost one cube even under a translation. The new kernel regression reproduced this before the fix: expected two solids, observed one.

The adapter now uses `ShapeFix_Shape` on the complete transformed result, then performs the existing whole-result BRep validity check and snapshot. The two-cube fixture tests translation, nonuniform scale, and reflection with independent expected solid counts, volumes and bounds; it checks that the input is unchanged. A controller fixture saves and reopens a two-cube compound with translation placement, then uses Boolean Union with a third disjoint cube: the result contains three solids, volume 3 mm³ and expected bounds, and one Undo and Redo restore the document states.

Validation: kernel transform suite 12 passed; kernel Boolean suite 79 passed and one known affine Union case ignored; Solid controller regressions 79 passed; Solid integration 198 passed/1 ignored; Solid workflows 114 passed/1 ignored. The old ignored nonuniform affine Union reproducer was explicitly rerun after this change and still fails with `BRep face has no tessellation`; its guard remains necessary. These fixtures do not establish preservation of every mixed compound, nested topology, imported model, transient native-memory bound, or desktop behavior. No new desktop executable was built in this validation.
