## Limits

Ellipse is a snapshot. Source does not require History; History requests
explicitly reject. Coincident foci/collinear FromFoci inputs cannot determine
the requested plane and reject recoverably. Sampled approximation metadata is
not a certified continuous Hausdorff bound. Full Matrix/Rhino editing, layer
inheritance and automatic topology correspondence are not accepted here.
