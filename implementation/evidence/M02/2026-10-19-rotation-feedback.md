Date: 2026-10-19. The user reported a bright yellow streak appearing only while rotating, and confirmed the screenshot came from OM9. The exact executable and gesture in that screenshot were not captured. Investigation reproduced a concrete renderer defect in the rotation-angle overlay; this does not assert that every line in the supplied screenshot was independently reconstructed.

The angle sector used 48 separately antialiased translucent triangle paths, plus one outline. Shared interior fringes blended repeatedly: an interior sample intended to have alpha 24 measured 30.517666 through the real egui tessellator. This removes internal antialias spokes and collapsed overlays; normal Gumball handles and the selected-object preview retain their existing behavior. No geometry, transform math, commit/Undo or source/topology rules changed. This is a rendering correction within the existing transient-angle feedback behavior, not a new reusable business rule requiring another skill approval.
