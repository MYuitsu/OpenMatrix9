# Polygon

Key **G**. A regular polygon.

![A regular hexagon: the constraints keep it regular, and the size of its circle sets its size.](img/sketch-polygon.png)

## How to draw it

1. Press **Polygon** and set in the bar above the **Sides** — 3 to 12 — and the way (below).
2. Click the centre (or the first end of a side), then the second point.

The ways:

- **inscr.** — the second click places a vertex: the polygon is inscribed in the circle;
- **circum.** — the second click places the middle of a side: the polygon is circumscribed about the circle;
- **by edge** — two clicks place the ends of one side.

The tool stays in hand — you can draw the next one. **Esc** puts it down.

## What you get

The sides are tied by equal lengths and angles: the polygon stays regular, and a centre and one size define it.

## Tip

A hexagon across flats is easiest drawn **circum.**: the circle touches the middles of the sides, and its diameter is
exactly the size across flats.
