# Circular pattern

![Eight copies round a circle: the distance from the axis is set by the part itself.](img/part-array-circular.png)

## How to do it

1. Select a body and press **Circular pattern** — or press the button and click the body.
2. In the bar above: **Copies** — how many round the circle; **Axis** — **Z (world)** by default. Click its button and
   pick a datum axis, a straight edge, a cylindrical face (its axis is taken) or two points in turn. The **Z** button
   brings the world axis back.
3. **Full circle (360°)** spreads the copies evenly round the whole circle. Untick it — the **Angle** of a sector
   appears at the geometry: say, four holes on a quarter of a flange.
4. **Enter** applies, **Esc** cancels.

An axis taken from the part follows it: the pattern stays coaxial after any edit.

## If it did not work

- The axis is not taken — what you clicked is not a straight edge, a cylinder or a datum axis. Take the axis by two
  points.
- The copies went to the wrong place — the wrong axis is chosen: look at what the bar says at **Axis**, and pick again.
