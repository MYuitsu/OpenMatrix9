## Custom Rail Advanced
• Complete the Custom Rail Advanced command.
• Next, select the planar Shape curve and turn
Control Points ON.
• Turn Planar ON in the Snaps menu. This ensures
when Controls Points are moved that they remain
planar.
• In the Looking Down Viewport select point(s) to
move.
• See the Custom Rail Advanced results change as
you are manipulating the control point positions.
• Press ESC key to turn off the Control Points and
complete editing.
