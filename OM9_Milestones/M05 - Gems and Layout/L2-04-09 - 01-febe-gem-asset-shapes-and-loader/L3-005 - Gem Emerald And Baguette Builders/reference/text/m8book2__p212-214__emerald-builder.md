### Size Mode:
Pavilion Rows: Sets the number of
rows in the pavilion of your custom
gem will have. Adjust in the builder
by selecting the Pavilion Rows icon
at the left in the builder and running
the slider below-or typing a number between 0
and 6 in the box.
### Editing Dimensions:
## Emerald Builder
‘gvEmerald’
Length: Sets the desired length of
the emerald. Adjust in the builder by
selecting the Length icon at the left
in the builder and running the slider
below-or typing a dimension in mm
in the box. You can also use the Yellow Horizontal
Viewport Control Handle on the girdle of the gem.
Create custom emerald shapes to meet precise
dimensions.
Where is this Command:
Access this command from Gems>Emerald
Builder
Steps:
Adjust the length of the Emerald using slider
or viewport control  handle.
• Open the Emerald Builder from them Gem Menu.
• Click on New Emerald button in the builder or
input a existing gem into the gem input box.
• Make any adjustments that you desire to
the preview using the builder or the control
handles.
• Press Enter to set the gem.
Width: Sets the desired width of
the emerald. Adjust in the builder by
selecting the Width icon at the left in
the builder and running the slider
below-or typing a dimension in mm
in the box.  You can also use the Yellow Horizontal
Viewport Control Handle on the girdle of the gem.

### Move:
Depth: Sets the desired depth of
the emerald from the table to the
culet. Adjust in the builder by
selecting the Depth icon at the left in
the builder and running the slider
below-or typing a dimension in mm in the box.
You can also use the Yellow Vertical Viewport
Control Handle on the table of the gem.
Move your emerald to position while the builder is
still active. This allows you to create your custom
emerald in place.
Adjust the depth of the Emerald using slider
or viewport control  handle.
Girdle Thickness: Sets the desired
girdle thickness of the emerald.
Adjust in the builder by selecting the
Girdle Thickness icon at the left in
the builder and running the slider
below-or typing a dimension in mm in the box.
You can also use the Orange Vertical Viewport
Control Handle on the girdle of the gem.
Adjust the girdle of the Emerald using slider
or viewport control  handle.
Move the emerald into position.

## Baguette Builder
‘gvBaguette’
Create custom baguette shapes to meet precise
dimensions.
### Modes:
The Baguette Builder has three modes; Size,
Move and Rotate. Each mode will be covered
individually.
Where is this Command:
Access this command from Gems>Baguette
Builder
Steps:
Size: This mode gives you control over the
dimensions of the baguette.
• Open the Baguette Builder from them Gem Menu.
• Click on ‘New Baguette’ button in the builder or
input a existing gem into the gem input box.
